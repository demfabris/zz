job with interval 2 and asserts the drawn DATE changes at seconds 1, 3, 5 and 7.

    AssertionError(('drawn DATE changes at seconds 1, 3, 5 and 7', 3,
     1789312874182492703, b'...0:date*   DATE[1789312874182492703]\n'))

  at the gate tip (the landing in):   red 6 of 6

WHY IT IS NOT THIS LANE'S. Both sides of the landing are red on almost every
run; one BASE run in five came back green and nothing else did. Beyond the
count: the scenario is four lines and invokes neither refresh-client nor
lock-client nor any command that reaches resolve_attached_client or
resolve_client_target, so it runs the same code at the tip that it ran at
origin/main. The landing changes one error path into a success path for a
clientless refresh-client and lock-client and touches nothing else.
