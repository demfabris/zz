zz only: the sidebar the raw TUI reached with M-s is still reachable as a binding.
The pin has no focus-sidebar command, so this is a zz measurement, not a comparison.

$ zz bind-key -n M-s focus-sidebar
exit=0
$ zz list-keys -T root M-s
bind-key  -T root M-s focus-sidebar
exit=0
$ zz bind-key -n 'C-\' detach-client
exit=0
$ zz list-keys -T root 'C-\'
bind-key  -T root C-\\ detach-client
exit=0

The pin refuses the zz-native verb, which is why focus-sidebar is measured here
and not in the differential:
