# key=value lines the moment it arrives, -F keeps the waiter until its format
# is true over the payload, -l and -w list and wake waiters. pane-exited and pane-died carry the exit keys and
# client-session-changed the old and new session; polled from a fixture since
# they land asynchronously. The last rows print 3.8's payload keys for
# window-renamed (no session or pane), pane-exited and pane-title-changed (pane
# and window only), window-linked (with window_index), session-window-changed
# (new and old window) and window-pane-changed (new and old pane).

out: show-environment -g HOOKS_EVENTS_SINK
