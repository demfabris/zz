TUI-005 attempt-01, the rectangle-copy sabotage

THE QUESTION the review asked: the corpus now copies a rectangle, but can that
case FAIL? This run answers it against the corpus case itself, not against the
shipped --self-check (which carries its own rectangle sabotage).

THE MODIFICATION, on a throwaway copy of the fixture in a scratch directory,
never on the committed one:
  - the corpus was trimmed to `run_movement emacs` alone
  - one line was inserted between the rectangle case and its copy:
        record_native_formats "$table-rectangle-past-end-of-line"
      + type_side zz "$KEY_DOWN"
        type_both "$KEY_COPY"
    so the zz side copies THREE lines out of the rectangle and the pin two.

THE RESULT: exit 1, one DIFF, in the buffer channel, with both byte strings.
The pin also shows the emacs half of the trailing-newline rule here: its
rectangle stops at 43 bytes with no terminator, which is why the emacs
past-the-end case ASSERTS the buffer channel and only the vi one records it.

A FIRST SABOTAGE THAT DID NOT REPORT, and why that is correct: one extra
cursor-right (not cursor-down) on the zz side changed nothing, because the
rectangle already reaches past the end of both lines and both engines trim
the trailing blanks. The keys under test have to move a copied GLYPH for the
buffer channel to see them; one more empty column is not one.
