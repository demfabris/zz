================================================================

WHAT IS RECORDED
----------------

compat/tui-screen-diff.sh, checkpoint `default-fg`, recorded not asserted:

    "zz writes an explicit RGB foreground where the pin leaves the
     foreground default"

The checkpoint clears both screens, sets `status-style bg=colour4` on both
sides - a style that names a BACKGROUND and no foreground - and compares.

So the divergence is one branch: a style that NAMES no colour takes the
`None` arm and is painted as an explicit RGB fallback, where a style that
names `default` or `terminal` already takes the `\e[39m` arm two arms below.
The two spellings of "no colour I chose" are emitted differently.


IS EMITTING \e[39m THERE SAFE? NO, NOT AS A ONE-LINE CHANGE
-----------------------------------------------------------

This is the question the reviewer asked, and the answer is why the trace
stops here rather than turning into a fix.

`\e[39m` means the OUTER TERMINAL's default foreground. The `fallback` this
arm currently writes is `default_foreground`, which is zz's own
TerminalAppearance foreground - the measurement above shows it as
216;222;233. They are not the same colour and they are not the same
CONTRACT: one is chosen by the user's terminal, the other by zz's theme.

The raw TUI paints its own ground across the whole canvas. A cell that
takes `\e[39m` for its glyph while zz has painted a zz-theme background
under it gets the terminal's foreground on zz's background, which is the
one combination nobody chose and which can land unreadable - a light
terminal default on zz's dark ground. The explicit RGB fallback is what
keeps the pane body legible against a ground zz painted itself.

Meeting the pin here therefore is not "emit \e[39m at 2634". It needs a
decision about what a zz client's default foreground MEANS when the client
paints its own background, and that decision belongs beside
presentation:tui-status-row-theme-defaults, the recorded decision that
already says every TUI surface other than the status row keeps zz's theme.
Emitting \e[39m for the status row alone, where the pin's own row is what
zz is trying to reproduce, is the narrow version and is plausible; doing it
for the pane body is not, on this evidence.


DISPOSITION
-----------

Measured, traced to its line, and NOT fixed. It is not covered by
options.native-mode-styles or by any other accepted decision, so unlike the
copy-mode and view-surface indicator cells it does not have a home yet. The
gate raises it as a cycle-4 residual rather than leaving it unnamed, and it
is the second of the two divergences task 3 asked this lane to measure.
