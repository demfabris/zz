WHY
101 shipped in zz 0.8.0, tagged on main at fd3c64e4, before this lane landed.
This lane appends, after that tag:
  - one u32 colour class word per style on the Terminal lane's viewport
    payload and per appended style on its patch payload, after the kitty
    placements (crates/zz-protocol/src/terminal_codec.rs);
  - the `client-features-v1:<spec>` value token on ClientHello capabilities
    (crates/zz-protocol/src/message.rs).
Both are pure trailing appends with their consumer halves in the same commit,
which is why the lane and its reviewer both read the rule as satisfied at 101.
But the envelope version is a gate, not a negotiation: a released 0.8.0 client
passes the version check and then misdecodes the style dictionary of every
frame. So the version moves.
