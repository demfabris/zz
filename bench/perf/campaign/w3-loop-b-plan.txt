QUESTION: May step (b) retain temporary execution workers and their ServerState mutex access until (c)/(e)? The literal 'Mutex taken only on the loop' requirement conflicts with this staging: wait_for blocks at daemon.rs:11818, client_file_operation blocks at :17041, format monitors execute hooks at :5669, and pane watchers acquire the mutex at :25525. These six slices preserve existing command stacks outside the loop. Enforcing sole loop ownership at the end of (b) requires bringing the relevant work from (c)/(e) forward.
QUESTION: Does the thread-removal requirement apply to Unix daemon sockets during this lane, allowing Windows named pipes to retain their existing backend? LocalListener::accept explicitly creates blocking streams at transport.rs:185, and TransportStream exposes neither general readiness registration nor nonblocking setup. Requiring the same result for Windows needs a separate named-pipe readiness implementation; an unconditional Unix descriptor requirement would also break other transports.
