## What this gate verified of TUI-014, and what it did not

TUI-014 stays **active**. Clause 1 asserts whole; clauses 2 and 3 are untouched
by this lane and the gate did not attempt them.

**Clause 1, re-measured at this gate's revision.** compat/tui-choosers.sh
exercises choose-client end to end and ends "all 78 asserted comparisons
identical, 0 recorded not asserted (0 for a sibling lane)" on each of three runs
at this revision (choosers-run-1.txt, -2.txt, -3.txt, each stamped with the
revision, the wall clock and the exit code). Inside that run the client case
asserts the row's key column and the client name, the `#{t/p:client_activity}:
session #{session_name}` text, the preview of that client's current pane, the
info preview `i` raises and withdraws, the four sort orders name, size, creation
and activity and their reversals, the filter prompt and its cancellation, the
help box at 100x40, and t, T, C-t and the `choose-client -Z` run with the pin's
default command. `client-tree-open` is asserted, not recorded.

**The two masks clause 1's evidence rides on were both defective and are both
fixed at this gate** - that is the substance of the reviewer's two mask
must-fixes, and it is why clause 1's assertion is worth more now than it was at
the lane's tip. `client_info_mask` no longer collapses every run of two or more
spaces on a row it touched, so a client info view whose label column sits a few
cells off is a difference rather than a match; `client_row_mask` no longer
replaces everything between the client name and the next box edge with one
token, so a client row that differs in its window count or its attached flag is
a difference. gate-mask-probe.txt is the reviewer's own probe re-run verbatim
against the fixed masks. The zz-side info-view sabotage now runs under
`client_info_mask`, the mask the client-info-view checkpoints actually assert
under, so clause 1's evidence has a sabotage of its own for the first time.

**Clause 2 is short.** clock-mode, customize-mode and switch-mode are not
implemented and suspend-client and server-access are not compared against a
measured pin refusal. The reviewer confirmed on both binaries that all five
answer `unsupported command: <name>` at exit 1 on zz.

**Clause 3 is short.** compat/tui-client-commands.sh ends "all 61 asserted
comparisons identical, 37 recorded not asserted (0 for a sibling lane)" at this
revision (gate-client-commands.txt, exit 0), six of those recorded cases being
this obligation's, and no item in `commands.native-client-tools` closed here.

**For whoever dispatches clause 2:** the reviewer's structural finding is that
`command:switch-mode` lives in `clients.interactive-refresh` and `server-access`
in a third group, not in `commands.native-client-tools`. The punch list's group
attribution has to be fixed before that batch goes out, or the lane will be
asked to close items in a group that does not hold them.
