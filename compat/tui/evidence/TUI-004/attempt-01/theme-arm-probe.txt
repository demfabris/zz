WHY THE ARM DID NOT LAND, measured rather than argued:
  1. The roster is 21 names, not the 11 the cycle-18 handoff recorded: theme plus TEN dark-theme-*
     AND TEN light-theme-*, because server_client_update_theme_colours picks the dark or the light
     set per client from the resolved theme. TMUX_OPTION_CONSUMERS goes 118 to 139, not to 129.
  2. crates/zz-mux/src/compat_manifest_tests.rs enforces a PARTITION: the consumer roster and the
     option:<name> items tracked in compat/tmux-gaps.json must be disjoint and must together equal
     the 180 name catalog. All 21 names are items of the OPEN gap options.theme-palette. Adding
     them as consumers therefore forces closing 21 items of a gap this lane does not own, moving
     the tracked count 62 to 41 and the scope counts [15,44,41,18] to [36,44,41,18], and it would
     assert that zz consumes options it would still not act on until the wire half lands.
  3. The wire is postcard, which is not self-describing, so an appended StatusLine field is not
     tolerated by an older decoder and PROTOCOL_VERSION 99 to 100 is mandatory rather than
     optional. The version reaches clients/web, which is a SEPARATE cargo workspace with its own
     lockfile, and clients/ios and clients/macos through crates/zz-client-ffi and
     include/zz-client.h. None of those is built by this lane proof set, so a bump landed here
     could not be shown not to break them.

WHAT THE FIELD WOULD BE, for whoever takes it next:

  set so a client that has not had its first publication keeps today behaviour. The daemon side

  #{E:<dark|light>-theme-<name>} in the client own format context, parse with the same colour
  parser style.rs uses, keep colour 8 where it does not parse, and use the terminal column
  [0,7,7,0,2,3,1,4,6,5] when theme is terminal. server-client.c:1173 is the exact shape: option 1
  terminal, 2 light, 3 dark, 0 detect which follows the client own reported theme.
  The client side is crates/zz-tui/src/render.rs write_ground and resolve_tmux_colour, which today
  resolve TmuxColour::Theme through the hard-coded DARK_THEME_COLOURS X11 names and would instead
  re-dispatch on the published TmuxColour, which is what makes colour124 leave as 48;5;124m.
